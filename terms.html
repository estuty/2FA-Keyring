What 2FA Keyring is

2FA Keyring is a free authenticator that runs entirely in your web browser. It generates time-based one-time passwords (TOTP, RFC 6238) from setup keys you add, and stores those keys encrypted on your device. There are no accounts, and we don't operate any server that stores your data. How it works is described on the security page.

Your responsibilities

Because your keys exist only on your device, keeping access to them is in your hands. You're responsible for:

  • Your passphrase: choosing a strong passphrase and remembering it
  • Backups: exporting encrypted backups and keeping them somewhere safe
  • Recovery codes: keeping each service's recovery codes separately from this app, so you can sign in if you lose access to your vault
  • Your device: keeping your device, browser and extensions secure and up to date, and locking the vault when you step away
  • Your codes: checking each code before relying on it, including that your device's clock is set correctly

No recovery

We can't recover your passphrase, your keys or your vault.

We never have a copy of them. If you forget your passphrase, clear your browser's data, or lose your device without a backup, your keys can't be restored by us or anyone else. Use your services' recovery codes to regain access to your accounts.
